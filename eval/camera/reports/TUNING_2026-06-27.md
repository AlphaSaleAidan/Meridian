# Vision detector confidence tuning — 2026-06-27

Goal: tune the production person-detector's confidence threshold against labeled
footage rather than guessing. Ran the **real** `MeridianDetector` (yolo11n +
ByteTrack) over MOT17-09 (150 frames, IoU 0.5) on the CPU-only Contabo box,
sweeping the confidence floor.

## Sweep results (MOT17-09, 150 frames, yolo11n, CPU)

| confidence | Precision | Recall |   F1  |  MOTA | IDF1  | MOTP(IoU) |
|-----------:|:---------:|:------:|:-----:|:-----:|:-----:|:---------:|
| 0.25       |   0.694   | 0.731  | 0.712 | 0.402 | 0.688 |   0.835   |
| 0.30       |   0.718   | 0.728  | 0.723 | 0.434 | 0.700 |   0.838   |
| 0.35 *(old default)* | 0.740 | 0.724 | 0.732 | 0.461 | 0.707 | 0.838 |
| 0.40       |   0.777   | 0.709  | 0.741 | 0.499 | 0.734 |   0.840   |
| **0.45 (new default)** | **0.823** | 0.688 | **0.750** | **0.535** | **0.744** | **0.840** |

## Read

As the confidence floor rises 0.25 → 0.45, precision climbs sharply (0.69 → 0.82)
while recall slips modestly (0.73 → 0.69). Crucially, the **aggregate** quality
metrics all improve monotonically: F1 +0.018, MOTA +0.074, IDF1 +0.037 versus the
old 0.35 default. For people-counting / dwell analytics, steadier tracks and fewer
false positives (higher precision, higher IDF1/MOTA) give more trustworthy counts
than chasing the last few distant/occluded detections.

## Change

- `src/camera/detector.py`: `DEFAULT_CONFIDENCE` 0.35 → **0.45**, overridable per
  deployment via `MERIDIAN_VISION_CONFIDENCE`. `MeridianDetector(confidence=...)`
  still wins when passed explicitly (the eval harness sets it directly).

## Caveats / next lever

- These are **yolo11n (nano) on CPU** numbers — the proof-of-correctness baseline,
  not the ceiling. The bigger win is the model: `yolo11m`/`yolo11x` or the planned
  RF-DETR swap should lift recall and MOTA materially. Re-run this sweep on the
  edge/GPU box after a model change to re-pick the threshold.
- Re-run: `python3 -m eval.camera.detect_track_eval --frames /tmp/mot17-09/img1
  --gt /tmp/mot17-09/gt.txt --name <tag> --confidence <c> --max-frames 150`.
