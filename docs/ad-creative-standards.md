# Meridian Ad Creative Standards

Reference guide for AI-generated video and image ads. Use these standards when crafting prompts, reviewing output, and building generation templates.

---

## Platform Specs

| Platform | Aspect | Optimal Length | Hook Window | File |
|----------|--------|---------------|-------------|------|
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 (1080x1920) | 7-15s awareness, 15-30s engagement | First 3s | MP4 |
| TikTok | 9:16 (1080x1920) | 9-15s max engagement, 21-34s recommended | First 2s | MP4/MOV |
| Facebook Feed | 4:5 (1080x1350) | Under 15s cold traffic | First 3s | MP4 |
| Instagram Feed | 1:1 (1080x1080) or 4:5 | 15-30s | First 3s | MP4 |

**Safe zones:** IG Reels: top 14%, bottom 35%, sides 6%. TikTok: top 160px, bottom 440px, sides 80px.

---

## The Hook (First 1-3 Seconds)

50% of viewers drop off in the first 3 seconds. Reels with 60%+ 3-second hold rate outperform by 5-10x.

**DO:**
- Open with surprising motion (splash, drop, reveal, sizzle)
- Direct question ("Ever wondered why...")
- Bold text overlay stating a problem
- Pattern interrupt (unexpected visual)

**DON'T:**
- Logo or brand name in first 2 seconds
- Wide establishing shot
- Slow fade-in or build-up
- Static product on shelf

**Formula:**
1. 0-3s: Native hook (motion + bold text)
2. 4-10s: Problem agitation
3. 11-20s: Product demo or social proof
4. Final 5-10s: Clear CTA

---

## Visual Pacing

- Cut every 2-3 seconds. Never hold a single shot for more than 3s in a sub-30s ad.
- Constant subtle motion (handheld camera, zooms, transitions).
- Sync text overlays with narration, 1.5-2s per text card.
- Native platform transitions (swipe, zoom, morph) feel organic.
- Fast cuts and jump cuts are preferred on TikTok.

## Color & Lighting

**Color rules:**
- Warm colors (red, orange, yellow) capture attention 0.3s faster than cool colors.
- Limit to 3-4 colors: one dominant, one accent (CTA), one-two supporting.
- High saturation + strong contrast stops the scroll.
- Simplified color palettes achieve up to 21% lower CPC.

**By vertical:**
- **Food:** Warm reds/oranges/yellows stimulate appetite. White plates make food pop. Red food on teal/cyan backgrounds = maximum contrast.
- **Retail:** Bold, high-contrast. Accent colors for CTAs and price callouts.
- **Services:** Blues/greens for trust. Warm accents for urgency.

**Lighting:**
- Natural light or soft diffused artificial. Golden hour is ideal.
- Side lighting creates depth and texture.
- Bright, well-lit scenes always outperform dark footage.
- Avoid harsh overhead fluorescents.

## Copy & Captions

**Top frameworks:**

| Formula | Structure | Best For |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| PAS | Problem, Agitate, Solution | Pain-point products |
| AIDA | Attention, Interest, Desire, Action | Product launches |
| 4Ps | Promise, Picture, Proof, Push | Premium products |

**Rules:**
- First 1-2 lines must hook before "see more" truncation.
- Short captions are more shareable.
- End with specific CTA: "Tap the link," "Comment MENU below," "Save this for later."
- Separate CTA with a line break.
- Engagement CTAs (questions) outperform link-click CTAs for reach.

---

## Hashtags

- **3-5 per post** (both IG and TikTok).
- Niche hashtags (<500K posts) outperform mega hashtags by 3x.
- TikTok 3-3-3 formula: 3 broad + 3 niche + 3 content-specific.
- Fewer targeted > many generic. Algorithms penalize hashtag spam.

---

## CTA Placement

- In-video: final 5-10 seconds, reinforced with on-screen text.
- Caption: end of caption, visually separated.
- Use platform-native CTA buttons (Shop Now, Learn More, Book Now).
- Never lead with CTA. Build value first.
- Specific beats generic: "Order the Triple Dipper" > "Check us out."

## Food / Restaurant Ads

**What goes viral:**
- Cheese pulls (Chili's sold 41M Triple Dippers driven by social cheese pull virality)
- Sizzle shots (close-up, audio-rich, food hitting hot surface)
- ASMR (exaggerated crunch, sizzle, slurp — drives repeat viewing)
- Steam and pour shots (slow-mo steam rising, sauce pouring)
- Assembly process videos (dish built from start to finish)

**Camera work:**
- **Close-up/macro:** Textures, cheese pulls, sizzle, steam. Shoot 120fps+ for smooth slow-mo.
- **Overhead/flat lay:** Plated dishes, ingredient layouts, assembly.
- **45-degree:** Most natural dining perspective, works for most dishes.
- **Wide:** Sparingly, only for ambiance. Never open with wide.
- **Mix angles:** Alternate overhead, side, and close-up for variety.

**Prompt template (food):**
```
Close-up slow-motion [FOOD ACTION] with [LIGHTING DESCRIPTION],
[TEXTURE DETAIL], warm color palette, shallow depth of field,
[CAMERA MOVEMENT], cinematic food photography style
```

Examples:
- "Close-up slow-motion cheese pull on a gourmet burger, warm golden hour kitchen lighting, steam rising, melted cheddar stretching, slight handheld camera movement, cinematic food photography"
- "Overhead shot of fresh pasta being plated, olive oil drizzle catching the light, herbs falling in slow motion, warm restaurant ambiance, soft bokeh background"
- "Extreme close-up of a steak sizzling on a cast iron skillet, fat crackling, smoke rising, warm side lighting, shallow depth of field, ASMR-style"

## Retail / Service Ads

**Before & After:**
- Split-screen or sequential reveal.
- "Before" for 2-3s max, then dramatic reveal.
- Contrast must be dramatic — subtle differences don't stop the scroll.
- Works for: cleaning, home improvement, beauty, fitness, landscaping, pools.

**Testimonial Scenes:**
- Customer testimonial usage rose from 17% to 47% (2023-2026).
- 82% of people say watching a video influenced their purchase decision.
- Real customers/employees, not actors.
- Structure: problem (3s) > experience (10s) > result (5s).

**Product Spotlight:**
- Isolate against clean background.
- Show in use, not just static.
- 360-degree rotation or multi-angle rapid cuts.
- Include a human hand for scale.

**Prompt template (retail):**
```
[PRODUCT/SERVICE] [ACTION/DEMONSTRATION], [SETTING],
professional lighting, clean composition, [CAMERA STYLE],
commercial quality, [MOOD/ENERGY]
```

## Image Ad Specs

| Platform | Best Size | Format |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Instagram Feed | 1080x1350 (4:5) | JPEG/PNG |
| Instagram Stories | 1080x1920 (9:16) | JPEG/PNG |
| Facebook Feed | 1080x1350 (4:5) | JPEG/PNG |
| Facebook Link | 1200x630 (1.91:1) | JPEG/PNG |

**Composition:**
- Rule of thirds: main subject on grid intersections.
- Leave negative space for text overlays.
- Leading lines toward CTA area.
- Text coverage under 20% of image area (Meta soft rule).
- High-contrast text: white on dark overlay, dark on light overlay.
- Sans-serif fonts at large sizes for mobile readability.

**Prompt template (image):**
```
Professional [PRODUCT/SCENE DESCRIPTION], [COMPOSITION STYLE],
[LIGHTING], high resolution, [COLOR PALETTE], commercial photography,
[BACKGROUND], sharp focus on [SUBJECT]
```

## AI Generation Tips (fal.ai)

**Model selection by use case:**
- **Cinematic commercial:** Veo 3.1, Kling v3 Pro, Seedance 2.0
- **Quick social content:** Seedance 2 Fast, MiniMax Hailuo
- **Budget/testing:** LTX Video, Wan 2.5

**Prompt engineering for AI video:**
- Include: product description, action, environment, cinematography direction, color palette.
- Detailed prompts outperform vague ones by 25-40% in CTR.
- Specify camera movement explicitly: "slow dolly in," "handheld tracking shot," "static close-up."
- Specify lighting: "warm golden hour," "soft diffused studio light," "dramatic side lighting."
- Specify mood: "energetic and vibrant," "calm and sophisticated," "appetizing and indulgent."

## Common Mistakes

1. Wrong aspect ratio (16:9 on Reels/TikTok)
2. Logo-first opening
3. Overproduced corporate feel
4. Ignoring safe zones
5. No sound design (silent ads -35% performance)
6. Same creative too long (ad fatigue)
7. Too much text overlay
8. Slow openings
9. Low resolution (below 1080p)
10. Generic hashtag spam
