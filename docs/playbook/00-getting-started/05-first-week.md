# First Week Checklist

Five days from zero to your first booked demos. Hit every item.

## Day 1 — Foundation

- [ ] Read **00-getting-started/** start to finish (you're 80% there if you've made it here)
- [ ] Read **50-cheatsheets/objection-handlers.md** twice
- [ ] Read **50-cheatsheets/discovery-questions.md** — memorize the 5 questions
- [ ] Open the demo dashboard: `meridian-dun-nu.vercel.app/demo` — click every tab, find the Money Left on Table page, click into a finding
- [ ] Run through `30-features/_index.md` so you know what every feature actually is (you will get asked)
- [ ] Confirm portal login works, you can see the Leads tab, you can add a lead

## Day 2 — Target identification

- [ ] Pick a city / neighborhood you'll work this week
- [ ] List 30 target businesses. Mix:
  - 10 restaurants/cafes
  - 10 smoke shops or dispensaries (where legal)
  - 5 salons/spas
  - 5 retail/boutique
- [ ] For each, note (best-effort): POS system, # of locations, owner name if visible
- [ ] Add all 30 to the portal Leads tab in **Prospecting** stage
- [ ] Pull the route on a map — group by neighborhood to minimize travel

## Day 3 — Outreach (in-person)

- [ ] Visit 10 businesses in person — best time windows:
  - Restaurants: 2–4 PM (post-lunch, pre-dinner)
  - Smoke shops: 11 AM–1 PM (slow window)
  - Salons: Tue/Wed mid-morning
  - Retail: weekday morning before rush
- [ ] Use the 60-second pitch from `50-cheatsheets/objection-handlers.md`
- [ ] Goal: leave with the owner's name + email OR a demo booked
- [ ] Update every lead in the portal — even "talked to barista, owner in at 9am tomorrow" is signal

## Day 4 — Outreach (remote)

- [ ] Call or email the remaining 20 leads
- [ ] Email subject lines that work:
  - "Quick question about your [POS] data"
  - "[Business name] — 60 seconds?"
  - "Saw your patio is back — quick idea"
- [ ] Skip cold "free demo" subject lines — they get marked spam
- [ ] Goal: 5 booked demos by EOD

## Day 5 — Close the loop

- [ ] Follow up on every Day-3/Day-4 conversation that didn't book
- [ ] Confirm next week's demos: send calendar invites, include the demo URL (`meridian-dun-nu.vercel.app/demo`)
- [ ] Pipeline review with team lead — share the 30 leads, the booked demos, any blockers
- [ ] Identify what worked: which vertical converted, which neighborhood, which opener — double down next week

## Targets for week 1

- 30 leads added
- 30 first-touches logged
- 5 demos booked for week 2
- 0 promises you can't keep

## What week 2 looks like

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Mon | 20 new leads + warm-touch the 30 from week 1 that didn't book |
| Tue–Thu | Run the 5 demos. Target: 2 proposals sent, 1 close by Thursday EOD |
| Fri | Referral asks on the week-2 close. Pipeline review. Plan week 3. |

By end of month 2 you should have ~4 active accounts paying. That's CA$960+/mo recurring to you, growing every month.

## If you stall

Common week-1 stalls and the fix:

| Stall | Diagnosis | Fix |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| Can't book any demos | Outreach script is too generic | Re-read pitch. Lead with the POS name + dollar hook, not "free demo" |
| Booked demos no-show | No reminder sent | Text day-of + 1hr before. No-show rate drops 60%. |
| Demos go well, no close | Closing the wrong objection | Re-read discovery framework. Their real objection isn't price. |
| Closes but cancels in 30 days | POS wasn't actually live | Always confirm the POS is on the live list (`10-pos-integrations/_index.md`) before promising onboarding timeline |
