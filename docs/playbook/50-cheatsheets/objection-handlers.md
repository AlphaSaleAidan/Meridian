# Objection Handlers — Cheat Sheet

The most common pushbacks and the exact lines that work.

## Pricing objections

### "The monthly fee is too expensive."

> "I understand. Let me ask — if I could show you CA$3,000/month in revenue you're currently missing, would our monthly fee to capture that be worth it? That's what our AI typically finds in week one."

### "Can I try it for free?"

> "We don't offer free trials because the onboarding takes real work — we connect your POS, backfill 18 months of data, and run 30+ AI agents on it. But it's month-to-month, no contract. If you don't see value in month one, you cancel with one click."

### "Why is it more expensive than the US version?"

> "Pricing is set at parity value for the Canadian market. The product, features, and support are identical."

### "I need to think about it."

> "Totally fair. What specifically would you want to think through? I want to make sure I've answered everything."

(Then address the real objection they reveal — "thinking about it" is almost always a code for an unsurfaced concern.)

### "I'm locked into a contract with [competitor]."

> "When does it end? Let me show you the demo now so you know exactly what you're switching to when it's up. I'll follow up 30 days before your renewal."

## "I already have analytics" objections

### "Square/Clover/Toast gives me analytics built in."

> "[POS] tells you what happened. We tell you what to do about it — and exactly how much each action is worth. That's the difference between a dashboard and intelligence."

### "I already use MarketMan / Lightspeed Analytics / [other tool]."

> "[Tool] is good at what it does. We're broader: revenue, customers, staff, forecasting, inventory, and cameras — all from one connection. [Tool] is a feature; we're the whole picture."

### "I have a spreadsheet that does this."

> "Respect. How long does it take you each week? Most owners we talk to spend 4–6 hours a week on theirs. We give you the same insights plus 20+ more you wouldn't think to track — and it updates automatically every hour. What would you do with that 4–6 hours back?"

### "I use Power BI / Tableau already."

> "Power BI and Tableau are tools — they need a data team to set up and maintain. We're plug-and-play: connect your POS, get insights in 24 hours. No SQL, no dashboards to build."

## "I don't have time" objections

### "I'm too busy right now."

> "I get it. That's exactly why this exists — Meridian works in the background, tells you the 3 things you should do today, dollar amounts attached. Total time commitment: 5 minutes a day to review the actions. Want me to come back Thursday at 2 PM for a 15-minute demo?"

### "Now isn't a good time."

> "When is? I'll come back. What's a slow window for you?"

(Don't push past 1 reschedule. If they're avoiding, they're not interested.)

## "Show me proof" objections

### "Show me a real example, not a demo."

> "Fair. [If you have a case study, share it]. If not: 'I can't show you another business's data — that's their data. What I can do is connect your Square/Clover/Toast right now during this call, and within 24 hours you'll see your own real numbers. That's the proof.'"

### "Who else in [city/vertical] is using this?"

> "[If true:] [Business] down the street signed up last month — happy to connect you with them for a 5-minute reference call." **Only use if true.**

> "[If not true in their city:] We're growing fast in [adjacent city]; you'd be the first in [their city]. Early-mover advantage — usually the first business in a market gets the best onboarding attention."

## Tech / compatibility objections

### "I'm on [POS] — does that work?"

Check `10-pos-integrations/_index.md` before answering. Then:

- **Live POS:** "Yes, [POS] is live. Connection takes about 3 minutes. We backfill 18 months and you'll see insights in 24 hours."
- **Wave 1 building:** "[POS] is in active build, expected within 30 days. I can put you on the priority list. In the meantime, CSV upload works to give you a preview."
- **CSV-only:** "We work with [POS] via daily CSV upload — no live API today. You'll get daily insights instead of real-time, but the analytics are the same."
- **Not supported:** "Honest answer: [POS] doesn't work with us today. If you ever migrate to Square/Clover/Toast, we're ready. Want me to follow up in a few months?"

### "Do you connect to my [specific brand] cameras?"

Check `20-camera-integrations/_index.md`. Then:

- **Supported:** "Yes — connection takes 2 minutes via RTSP. You'll see foot traffic by end of week 1."
- **Not supported (Wyze/Nest/Arlo):** "Those don't expose the right stream type. Easy fix: $60 Reolink alongside what you have. We do analytics; your existing camera stays for mobile alerts."

### "Will this slow down my POS?"

> "No. We're read-only — pulling data from your POS, not pushing anything to it. The only thing you'll notice on the POS side is the connection itself in your settings. Zero performance impact."

### "What happens to my data?"

> "Encrypted in transit and at rest. Stored in Canada-region infrastructure for Canadian merchants. Never sold to third parties. You can delete and disconnect any time with one click. No human at Meridian reviews your individual data unless you request support."

## Decision-making objections

### "I need to talk to my partner/spouse/business partner."

> "Totally understand. Can we schedule a 10-minute call with both of you this Thursday? I want to make sure they see what you saw today."

### "I need to think about whether this is the right time."

> "Fair. Two questions to help you decide: (1) Are you trying to grow revenue? (2) Are you willing to spend 5 min/day on the actions our AI surfaces? If yes to both, the only question is whether you start in May or October — and every month you wait costs you the CA$2,800+/mo we're typically finding."

### "I don't trust AI."

> "I get it. Look at it this way — we're not replacing your judgment, we're surfacing patterns in your data so you can make better decisions. You still decide what to do. Want to see how an actual insight looks? It's specific: 'Tuesday discount is costing CA$240/mo, kill it.' That's not AI-mysticism, that's math."

### "My business is too small for this."

> "How small? [Hear them.] Our typical merchant is 1–3 locations doing CA$300K–CA$3M/year. If you're in that range, we move the needle. If you're under CA$200K, honest answer: the recurring cost is probably more than what we'd find for you in the first 90 days. I'd rather tell you that than waste both our time."

### "My business is too big for this."

> "How big? [Hear them.] If you're a multi-location chain under 20 stores, our multi-location add-on is built for you — cross-store comparison, location analyst, custom AI models. If you're enterprise (50+ stores, multiple verticals), we have an enterprise conversation through a different path — let me set that up."

## Closing objections

### "I'll get back to you."

> "Of course. When? I want to follow up on your timeline, not mine. Is Thursday good?"

(Pin a date. "I'll get back to you" without a date = lost lead 80% of the time.)

### "Let me sleep on it."

> "Smart. While you're sleeping on it, let me set up your connection so when you decide, we're not waiting on tech. 5 minutes now, you cancel before midnight if it doesn't feel right."

### "What's the catch?"

> "No catch. Month-to-month, no setup fee, one-click cancel. We make money when you stay — so our incentive is to make sure you see value. If we don't, you leave, and we lose. That's the model."

### "I want a discount."

> "I hear you. Two options: (1) Pay annually upfront — you get 2 months free. (2) Refer 3 other businesses who close — you get 50% off your monthly rate for as long as all 3 stay active. Which one fits?"

**Do not free-form discount.** Annual prepay and referral discounts only. Anything else, escalate to CS Manager.

## When to walk away

These signals mean it's not worth pushing:

- They've cancelled "everything" 3+ times in the conversation
- They want custom features built specifically for them
- They want a guarantee of specific dollar outcomes
- They want to negotiate the 70% commission structure (not your call anyway)
- They're on a POS that's not supported AND not on the roadmap

In all cases: polite no, leave the door open, move on. Walking away from bad-fit prospects is your most underrated skill.
