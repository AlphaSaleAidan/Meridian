# Discovery Questions — Cheat Sheet

Memorize the core 5. Use the vertical-specific deep-dives only after the merchant is engaged.

## The Core 5 (every discovery call, every time)

Ask these BEFORE showing anything. 2–3 minutes total.

1. **"What POS system are you on?"**
   - Drives the entire rest of the conversation
   - Confirms supported / wave-1 / CSV-only / unsupported
   - If unsupported, you may want to qualify out early
2. **"How many locations?"**
   - 1 → Standard or Premium
   - 2+ → Command (custom pricing, 70% commission to you — higher volume = larger deal)
3. **"What's your biggest operational headache right now?"**
   - Their answer tells you which feature to LEAD the demo with
   - Common answers: staffing, inventory, knowing what to push, customer retention
4. **"Do you currently track any analytics beyond what your POS dashboard shows?"**
   - "No" → you're their first analytics layer (easier sell, lower bar)
   - "Yes, [tool]" → competitive positioning needed
5. **"If you could know one thing about your business that you don't today, what would it be?"**
   - Pure gold. Tells you exactly which feature to demo.
   - Translate their answer:
     - "When my real busy hour is" → Peak Hours
     - "Which products to push" → Product Velocity + Menu Engineering
     - "Why I'm losing money" → Money Left on Table
     - "When my regulars are about to leave" → Customer LTV + Churn
     - "How my staff is actually performing" → Employee Performance

**Write their answers down. Reference them during the demo.** "You mentioned staffing was your biggest headache — look at this..."

## Deeper discovery (after the core 5, if they're engaged)

### Operations

- "Walk me through a typical day. What's your first hour like? Your peak? Your close?"
- "How do you decide how to staff?"
- "How do you decide what to order?"
- "Do you run promos? How do you know if they work?"

### Customers

- "What's a regular customer for you?"
- "When was the last time a regular stopped coming and you didn't know why?"
- "Do you have a loyalty program? How's it working?"

### Money

- "What's your biggest expense after rent and labor?"
- "Have you tested raising prices recently?"
- "What's your slowest day? Have you tried anything to fix it?"

### Cameras (Premium qualifier)

- "Do you have security cameras already?" (Yes → Premium upsell signal)
- "What brand?" (Hikvision/Dahua/Reolink/UniFi/Axis → easy; Wyze/Nest/Arlo → suggest add-on)
- "How many cameras and where?"
- "Have you ever thought about using them for analytics, not just security?"

### Decision-making

- "Who else weighs in on a decision like this — partner, spouse, business partner?"
- "How do you typically evaluate software for the business?"
- "What would need to be true for you to say yes today?"

## Vertical-specific discovery

### Restaurants

- "Do you know your real food cost percentage, or are you guessing?"
- "Which menu items are actually making you money vs. just selling well?"
- "How much do you lose to no-show reservations?"
- "Patio season — how do you predict the staffing?"

### Smoke shops

- "How much shrinkage do you see each month?"
- "Which SKUs are your fastest movers? Slowest?"
- "When you reorder, are you guessing or do you have a method?"

### Cannabis

- "What's your average ticket and how has it trended last 6 months?"
- "How do you track patient/customer retention?"
- "What's your weakest day of the week?"
- "Any compliance or PII concerns we should discuss upfront?"

### Salons / spas

- "What's the average gap between visits for your regulars?"
- "How do you know when a regular hasn't rebooked?"
- "Which services are most often booked together?"
- "Which stylist/therapist drives the highest repeat business?"

### Retail / boutiques

- "How much dead stock are you sitting on right now?"
- "How do you decide what to reorder?"
- "Do you know which products sell together?"
- "When do you mark down — and is that timing working?"

### Food trucks / QSR

- "What's your busiest 15-minute window?"
- "How do you decide which days to skip a location?"
- "Do you change pricing for events?"

### Automotive

- "Are you tracking technician productivity?"
- "How much parts inventory is sitting more than 90 days?"
- "What's your labor margin vs. parts margin?"
- (Note: vertical is CSV-only — set expectations accordingly)

## Red-flag answers (qualify out)

| Answer | What it means | Action |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| "I run my business on cash, no POS" | Can't connect | Polite decline |
| "I don't really track anything" + small revenue | Low fit, will churn fast | Pass or sell Standard with no upsell |
| "I want a guarantee that I'll make X back" | Wants contractual ROI promise | Decline; we don't guarantee outcomes (compliance issue) |
| "I want you to build me a custom thing" | Wrong model | Polite no; not a service business |
| "My partner makes all decisions, I'm just curious" | Not the buyer | Get the partner on a call or move on |

## How to actually run the discovery call (timing)

- **0–2 min:** Rapport. "How long you been in business? How'd you get started?"
- **2–5 min:** The core 5 questions. Write answers down.
- **5–10 min:** 2–3 deeper questions in the area they care most about.
- **10–25 min:** Demo — emphasize the features that match their #3 (biggest headache) and #5 (thing they wish they knew).
- **25–30 min:** Close. Recommend a plan. Assumptive close: "Which makes more sense — Standard or Premium?"

Total: 30 minutes. Don't run long. Most demos that go past 45 min lose.
