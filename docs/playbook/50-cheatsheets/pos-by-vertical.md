# POS by Vertical — Cheat Sheet

The first question on every cold call should be "what POS are you on?" The second should be vertical-confirmation. This table tells you what to expect at the intersection.

## Restaurants & Cafes

| POS | Status | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| **Toast** | LIVE | #1 restaurant pitch; rich data; camera add-on natural fit (most restaurants have cameras) |
| **Square** | LIVE | Cafes, small restaurants, food trucks; easiest connect |
| **Clover** | LIVE | Casual restaurants; token-refresh bug pending |
| **Lavu** | WAVE 1 BUILDING | Independent restaurants, bars |
| **Lightspeed Restaurant** | WAVE 1 + partner | Hospitality, multi-loc |
| **CAKE** | WAVE 1 (CSV path) | Sysco channel; ~5k restaurants opportunity |
| **TouchBistro** | WAVE 2 partner | Canada wedge |
| **SkyTab** | WAVE 1 + partner | Revel migration tailwind (~18k merchants) |
| **SpotOn** | WAVE 2 partner (slow) | File on prospect signal |
| **talech** | WAVE 1 BUILDING | Came in through Elavon |

**Best opener:** "Do you know your real food cost percentage, or are you guessing?"

**Demo emphasis:** Menu engineering matrix, peak hour staffing, food waste tracking, customer churn (regulars).

**Avg deal:** Base product + camera intelligence add-on common where cameras are present (custom pricing).

**Decision maker:** Owner or GM. Franchise = franchisee, never corporate.

**Best visit window:** 2–4 PM (after lunch rush, before dinner prep).

## Smoke Shops & Vape

| POS | Status | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| **Clover** | LIVE | Dominant in smoke shops; lead with shrinkage angle |
| **Square** | LIVE | Smaller smoke shops, vape shops |
| **Korona** | WAVE 1 BUILDING | Specialty retail incl. some smoke shops |
| **Shopify POS** | WAVE 1 BUILDING | Online + physical hybrid |

**Best opener:** "How much are you losing to shrinkage every month? Most smoke shop owners don't know until inventory day — and by then it's too late."

**Demo emphasis:** Waste/shrinkage (anomaly detection on voids), product velocity, inventory intelligence.

**Avg deal:** Base product (custom pricing).

**Decision maker:** Usually owner-operated.

**Best visit window:** 11 AM–1 PM (slow). Evening too busy.

**Tip:** Many smoke shops are on Clover. Lead with "Are you on Clover?" as your opening — instant credibility.

## Salons & Spas

| POS | Status | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| **Square** | LIVE | Most common in independent salons |
| **Clover** | LIVE | Some salons, especially with multiple stations |
| **Shopify POS** | WAVE 1 BUILDING | Hybrid product + service salons |

**Best opener:** "How do you know when a regular client is about to switch to another salon? Our AI flags it before their next appointment would have been."

**Demo emphasis:** Customer LTV + churn warning (THE feature for salons), staff performance comparison, basket analysis (service upsells).

**Avg deal:** Base product (custom pricing; single location, relationship-driven).

**Decision maker:** Salon owner; sometimes manager with buying authority.

**Best visit window:** Tuesday/Wednesday mid-morning (slowest days).

**Tip:** Bring a one-pager. Salons are visually driven — they convert when they can see the report.

## Food Trucks & QSR

| POS | Status | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| **Square** | LIVE | THE food truck POS |
| **Clover** | LIVE | Some QSR |
| **Toast** | LIVE | Fast-casual / QSR with seating |

**Best opener:** "Your busiest hour makes or breaks your day. Do you know exactly when your peak is — down to the 15-minute window?"

**Demo emphasis:** Peak hour optimizer (critical for food trucks), revenue forecasting (predict slow days), menu engineering (push items during rush).

**Avg deal:** Base product (custom pricing; budget-conscious solo operators).

**Decision maker:** Almost always the owner.

**Best approach:** Visit at the truck during a slow period, or DM on Instagram (food truck owners live on Instagram).

**Unique sell:** "You probably track sales in your head. What if your phone showed you tomorrow's predicted revenue, your best-selling items by location, and exactly when your rush starts — automatically?"

## Automotive (entire vertical = CSV-only)

Vendors: Mitchell1, ALLDATA, NAPA TRACS, TireMaster, R.O. Writer, Protractor, Bolt On, AutoVitals, AutoFluent, Omnique.

**All of these are CSV-only.** No live API today. Demo limited to what's in the CSV.

**Best opener:** "Auto repair / tire shops — biggest margin question is parts-vs-labor and technician productivity. We pull your daily sales CSV and tell you which technicians are underutilized and which parts are dead inventory. Most shops find 8–12% margin recovery."

**Demo emphasis:** Product velocity (parts dead stock), employee performance (technician productivity), revenue forecasting, day-of-week patterns.

**Avg deal:** Base product (custom pricing; CSV-only limits depth, so don't push camera add-on).

**Decision maker:** Shop owner.

## Universal — what to ALWAYS confirm in discovery

Regardless of vertical:

1. POS system + version (some have multiple variants)
2. Number of locations (1 = base product ± camera add-on; 2+ = multi-location add-on candidate)
3. Have they used analytics before? (sets baseline expectations)
4. Owner vs manager (decision maker confirmation)
5. Cameras already installed? (camera intelligence add-on signal)
