# Peak Basket

> Group: **cross-reference**
> Tier: **Tier 3**
> Plan: **Premium+**

## What it does

Correlates traffic density (busy vs quiet) with basket size and composition.

## What it needs

Cameras + POS.

## Example output

> When >15 people in-store, avg ticket drops 18% (customers grab + go; don't browse impulse). Stage your impulse items at checkout.
