# Product Placement

> Group: **cross-reference**
> Tier: **Tier 4**
> Plan: **Premium+**

## What it does

Optimizes product positioning by cross-referencing zone traffic with sales velocity.

## What it needs

Cameras + POS + zone-to-product mapping.

## Example output

> High-margin item #A is in low-traffic zone (12% of footfall). Move to checkout zone (78% footfall): projected +CA$340/mo.
