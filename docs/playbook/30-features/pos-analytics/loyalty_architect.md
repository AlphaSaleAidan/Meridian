# Loyalty Architect

> Group: **pos-analytics**
> Tier: **Tier 3**
> Plan: **Standard+**

## What it does

Loyalty program design, redemption rates, reward ROI, retention impact.

## What it needs

Customer IDs + existing loyalty data (where merchant has it) or hypothesis modeling.

## Example output

> Buy-10-get-1-free: 38% redemption, retention lift +14%, ROI 3.2x. Increase reward to buy-8 — projected retention +6% more.
