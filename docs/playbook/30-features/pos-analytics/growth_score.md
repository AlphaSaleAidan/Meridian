# Growth Score

> Group: **pos-analytics**
> Tier: **Tier 5**
> Availability: **Base product (all merchants)**

## What it does

Executive growth-opportunity score 0–100 with a monthly action plan (rolls up all other agents).

## What it needs

Outputs from tier 1–4 agents.

## Example output

> Growth Score: 64/100. Top 3 actions this month: (1) launch Tuesday lunch combo, (2) reorder dead-stock SKUs, (3) staff +1 on Sat lunch.

## Where it lives

Source: `src/ai/agents/growth_score.py` or `src/camera/growth_score.py` (vision modules) or `src/ai/growth_score.py` (coordination)
