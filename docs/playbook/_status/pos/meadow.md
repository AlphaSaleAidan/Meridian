## What it is
A San Francisco-founded (2014), California-focused cannabis dispensary POS covering retail checkout, delivery dispatch, ecommerce menus, Metrc compliance, and CRM — positioned as the boutique alternative to Dutchie for CA operators.

## Vertical & market
- **Primary vertical:** Cannabis dispensary (state-regulated retail + delivery)
- **NA market presence:** Medium — meaningful concentration in California; expansion footprint in MI, MA, NY, NJ, and DC. Self-claims G2 #1 cannabis POS but trails Dutchie and Treez in raw operator count.
- **Typical merchant profile:** Single-location California dispensary, delivery-only operator, consumption lounge, or small multi-location MSO
- **Geographic concentration:** US-only (federally illegal — no Canada/intl). CA-heavy.

## How to spot the merchant uses it
- Budtender ringing up sales on an iPad with the Meadow app; admin URL `admin.getmeadow.com`
- Delivery-only operator (Meadow leans into compliant CA delivery)
- "Powered by Meadow" on the dispensary's ecommerce menu
- Operator name-drops Meadow, "the Meadow team," or mentions Metrc + Nabis + Weedmaps all wired together
- California-licensed, often boutique / founder-operated

## Connect flow (what the merchant does)
1. Meridian emails `partners@getmeadow.com`, gets API spec + permission for the X-Api-Key path
2. Dispensary admin logs into `admin.getmeadow.com`, goes to `Settings > API > Add Integration > Meridian`, generates key
3. Meadow support finalizes activation (per documented pattern for other partners)
4. Merchant pastes key into Meridian connect screen; we test against `/dispensaries` before unlocking dashboard

## What blocks LIVE status today

## Common failure modes
- **401 on every call** → key sent as `Bearer` or `Authorization` → confirm `X-Api-Key: <key>` header literal
- **404 on `/orders` or `/patients`** → endpoint path drift between registry and live spec → revalidate with Meadow partner team
- **Empty results despite live sales** → wrong dispensary scope on key (multi-location operator) → ask merchant to regenerate scoped to correct location

## Strategic notes
Meadow is the right second-tier cannabis play after Dutchie/Treez — same vertical-specific cost (no Stripe, patient PII, federal Schedule I), but a smaller, more partner-friendly company with a real partnerships team and a published email contact. If Meridian is already committing to cannabis (Dutchie + Treez), adding Meadow captures the CA boutique/delivery segment those two underserve. Standalone (without Dutchie/Treez), Meadow alone won't justify the cannabis compliance overhead.
