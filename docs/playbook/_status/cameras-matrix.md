## Rep heuristic (field cheat-sheet)

1. **If it's PoE/wired and the badge is Axis, Hikvision, Dahua (or its OEMs — Amcrest, EmpireTech, LaView, ANNKE, older Lorex/Honeywell), UniFi, Reolink, Bosch, Avigilon, Verkada, or N-series Lorex** → you can integrate. Get on the LAN, log into the camera/controller, enable RTSP, and grab the URL using the brand pattern in the matrix above. UniFi is the exception — it's `rtsps://` on port 7441. Verkada is LAN-only, RFC1918 source addresses only. Avigilon/Bosch usually require coordinating with the merchant's integrator.

2. **If it's eufy wired (Indoor / SoloCam wired / Floodlight wired) or a Swann wired DVR/NVR** → probably workable, but treat as BEST EFFORT. Verify the SKU before promising integration. For Swann, identify the underlying OEM (Raysharp / Hikvision / Dahua) before picking a URL pattern.

3. **If it's Wyze, Nest, Arlo, Ring, eufy battery/doorbell, Swann Wi-Fi/battery (SwannSecurity / Tracker), Lorex W-series standalone Wi-Fi, or any standalone battery Reolink (Argus without Home Hub)** → it's a hard no. Don't promise an integration. Pitch a $60 Reolink RLC-510A or Hikvision DS-2CD2043G2-I PoE camera alongside the consumer gear they already own — they keep the mobile app alerts, we get the analytics stream.

4. **If it's Camect** → it's not a camera, it's a hub. Open the Camect app, identify the cameras Camect is ingesting, and integrate directly with those cameras using the relevant brand playbook. Meridian and Camect can both read the same camera RTSP simultaneously.

5. **If the customer is federal, federally-funded, or critical infrastructure** → only Axis, Bosch, Avigilon, and Verkada work (NDAA Section 889 blocks Hikvision, Dahua, Amcrest, and Lorex — including the post-Skywatch Lorex, since components are still Dahua-sourced). For everyone else, NDAA is a non-issue — confirm during qualification only if they mention federal contracts.

6. **Don't know the brand?** Walk the diagnostic flow in [_how-to-connect-any-camera.md](./_how-to-connect-any-camera.md). ONVIF auto-discovery + vendor app inspection + the URL patterns in this matrix will cover 90% of unknown cameras inside 10 minutes.
