# Arlo — NOT SUPPORTED

> Status: **NOT SUPPORTED**
> Why: Cloud-only encrypted streams. No RTSP. No ONVIF. No local stream access of any kind.

## What to say when a merchant asks

> "Arlo doesn't expose any way to get the video stream outside their app — it's encrypted, cloud-only, no RTSP, no ONVIF, no local access. There's nothing on our side or theirs that fixes that. The fix is to add a $60 Reolink at the entrance for analytics and keep the Arlo for what it's good at (mobile alerts, recording for theft review)."

## Why we don't support it (technical)

- Arlo streams are end-to-end encrypted to the Arlo app
- No public API for partners to access streams
- No local stream protocol (RTSP, ONVIF, etc.)
- No third-party integration that bypasses this — by Arlo's design

Even Arlo's own "for business" tier doesn't expose stream access to analytics partners.

## What we sell instead

Recommend: **Reolink RLC-510A** ($60) or **Hikvision DS-2CD2043G2-I** ($80–$100).

Same script as Wyze and Nest — add a cheap PoE camera alongside the consumer camera. Two-camera setup, $60 hardware, immediate analytics.

## Sales math

- Arlo already deployed: $0
- Reolink alternative: $60 one-time
- Premium tier upsell: $239/mo commission recurring

## Watch out

Some merchants invested heavily in Arlo for a whole-business setup (4–8 cameras). They may not want to add yet another camera system.

Honest play:

> "If you're committed to Arlo-only, the analytics piece won't work today. POS analytics still does (Standard plan). I won't sell you Premium if cameras aren't going to deliver — bad for both of us."

Don't sell Premium to an Arlo-only merchant. They'll churn in 30 days and you'll lose the recurring commission anyway. Sell Standard, and if they're open to a single Reolink later, upsell then.
