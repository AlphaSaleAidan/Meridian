## Findings (analyst notes, 2026-07-16)

1. **The `subscriptions` table is EMPTY (0 rows).** Live merchants are billed
   entirely outside the platform's own billing records (Square invoices /
   Square subscriptions created ad hoc), so *no* merchant's monthly price can
   be verified against their deal in-platform today. Every "no live
   subscription" row above is this finding, not a churned merchant.
2. **Zero lead → merchant linkage.** No closed lead carries structured fee
   terms and nothing connects a lead to the org it became; matching by email
   found 6 leads whose emails have a `businesses` row, 9 with none (several
   are seeded demo/test leads). `merchant_billing_terms.source_lead_id`
   closes this gap for every deal closed after this PR.
3. **Per-order fee coverage is 1 / 4.** Of 4 `phone_agent_config` rows, only
   one records a rep-negotiated `order_fee_cents`; the rest silently bill the
   `MERIDIAN_SERVICE_FEE_CENTS` env default regardless of what was sold.
4. **No overbilling was detectable — but that is "unverifiable", not
   "healthy".** With no in-platform record of contracted or applied monthly
   fees, over/underbilling of the live merchant base cannot be measured until
   `merchant_billing_terms` is populated (automatic for new deals after this
   PR; the admin override endpoint backfills existing merchants).

**Recommended follow-ups:** apply the two `20260716_*` migrations, backfill
`merchant_billing_terms` for the live merchant base via
`POST /api/billing/terms/{merchant_id}/override` (reason: `backfill_live_base`),
then re-run this audit — matched/mismatched become meaningful from that point.
